## The EPAT Journey

1

Instant Content Access & Onboarding

Confirm enrollment, log into the LMS within minutes, and receive a “next steps” playbook that outlines timelines, key contacts, and useful links, so you start strong.

2

Dedicated Support Manager

Your single point of contact for everything: technical queries, conceptual guidance, expert introductions, even pep talks before interviews.

[Know More](#personalised-learner-support)

3

EPAT Primers & Prep Sessions

Seven concise primers (Python, Statistics, Markets, etc.) plus live Q&A workshops prepare your foundation weeks before core lectures begin.

[Know More](/epat#curriculum)

4

Weekend Live Lectures & Assignments

Across six months, 20+ global practitioners teach 11 core modules. Post-class quizzes, coding tasks, and case studies reinforce each concept in real-time.

[View Curriculum](/epat#curriculum)

5

Hands-On Project Work

Choose mini-projects to practise new tools, then collaborate on a mentored capstone you can deploy in the live market (data pipelines, execution, risk controls, and more).

6

Mid-Term & Mock Exams

A multiple-choice mid-term at Month 2 highlights knowledge gaps early; a full mock plus best-practice workshop gets you “exam-ready” before the real thing.

7

EPAT Certification Exam

Proctored online or at 80+ centres worldwide. Passing earns the industry-recognised EPAT credential, proof of your quant trading skill set.

8

Alumni & Placement Support

Lifetime access to job alerts, résumé reviews, mock interviews, salary-negotiation tips, and direct introductions to 300+ hiring partners.
