## Financial Aid & Funding Opportunities

### IBF Funding under STS for Singaporeans

IBF - Institute of Banking and Finance is the national accreditation and certification agency for financial industry competency in Singapore. Read more about IBF [here](https://www.ibf.org.sg).

EPAT has been accredited under the IBF Standards, and is eligible for funding under the IBF Standards Training Scheme [(IBF-STS)](https://www.ibf.org.sg/home/for-individuals/skills-and-jobs-development/upskill/programme-finder/programme-detail?id=TGS-2022601284), subject to all eligibility criteria being met.

A participant must **successfully complete** the EPAT programme (including passing all relevant assessments and examinations) in order to be eligible.

Prospective participants are advised to assess the **suitability** of the programme and its **relevance** to participants’ business activities or job roles.

IBF - Institute of Banking and Finance is the national accreditation and certification agency for financial industry competency in Singapore. Read more about IBF [here](https://www.ibf.org.sg/home/for-individuals/skills-and-jobs-development/upskill/programme-finder/programme-detail?id=TGS-2022601284).

IBF-STS provides upto **50% funding** for direct training costs subject to a cap of **S$ 3,000 per candidate per programme** subject to all eligibility criteria being met.

This is applicable to **Singapore Citizens** or **Singapore Permanent Residents**, physically based in Singapore. Find out more on [www.ibf.org.sg](https://www.ibf.org.sg)

Participants originating from emerging countries are eligible for an exclusive subsidy based on their nationality. Check your eligibility by submitting a request.

#### What are Emerging Markets?

These are countries that have some characteristics of a developed economy but do not fully meet its standards. A country with a GDP per capita nominal below $35,000 is considered an Emerging Market.

## FAQs

##### How many batches run in a year?

* Historically, we have been running 4 batches of EPAT in a year.

##### Would I get a refund if I change my mind after enrolling?

* We provide an opportunity to clear all your doubts about the programme prior to enrollment. Also, you get access to a dedicated team support even before your EPAT programme starts. Therefore, we follow a no refund policy.

##### Can I change my batch after the payment has been made?

* Certainly, you have the opportunity to do so within 45 days from the commencement of the batch, and there won’t be any extra charges.

##### While pursuing my undergraduate/bachelor’s degree, can I apply and be accepted into EPAT?

* Yes, you can apply. College students can benefit from EPAT by making themselves “more recruitable” with the required practical skills in the financial markets domain. EPAT alumni include college students who bagged good roles after successful completion of their college degree and EPAT.
* Please note that while QuantInsti offers lifetime placement services to all EPAT alumni, it does not guarantee any placements.
* QuantInsti offers student discounts to all full-time students, which can be availed by sharing the valid college ID proof and other required documents.
* Send us your details by filling the application form [here](/admission-form) and we will arrange a counselling call for you.

##### Without programming or technology background, can I apply and be accepted into EPAT?

* Embark on your journey with EPAT with ease, even if you're new to programming! Once you enroll successfully, you'll gain access to our exclusive primer content. Designed for traders and financial market professionals without a programming background, these self-paced, interactive modules will guide you through the fundamentals of coding. They're the perfect way to build your foundation before diving into the more advanced and intricate concepts covered in our classroom sessions. Plus, with integrated tests in each module, you can effortlessly track and assess your progress in learning.

##### Without trading or financial markets experience, can I apply and be accepted into EPAT?

* Yes, you can apply. If you are from engineering or quantitative sciences background, you might already have some basic exposure to the skills required for Algorithmic trading domain. To check your eligibility in EPAT, apply for the scholarship test here. EPAT counsellors will get in touch with you and guide you further.For beginners in Quantitative domain, EPAT offers primer modules on Statistics, Econometrics, Options, Financial markets, Excel computation and Python programming along with assessment tests in each of these categories.
